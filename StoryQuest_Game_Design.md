# StoryQuest (Working Title)

## High Concept
A story-driven educational adventure designed for young children that blends the interactive call-and-response feel of *Dora the Explorer* with the progression of a light RPG.

The game is played in first-person or over-the-shoulder third-person. The player explores a semi-linear world where every obstacle is solved through reading, math, observation, logic, or empathy instead of combat.

Designed for projector play, the room becomes part of the experience.

---

# Core Pillars

- Learning never feels like homework.
- Story first, education second.
- Choices permanently shape the player's avatar.
- Multiple paths with a manageable branching narrative.
- Scales with the child's age.

---

# Gameplay Loop

1. Explore
2. Meet NPC
3. Story dialogue
4. Learning challenge
5. Player choice
6. World reacts
7. Avatar evolves
8. Continue adventure

---

# Avatar Evolution

No XP bars.

Instead, appearance reflects decisions.

Examples:
- Explorer Hat
- Forest Cloak
- Wizard Hat
- Crystal Armor
- Animal Companions

Traits:
- Brave
- Curious
- Kind
- Clever
- Creative

NPC dialogue changes depending on these traits.

---

# Projector Features

Project scenery fills the wall.

Occasional prompts encourage physical movement.

Examples:
- Point at letters.
- Count projected stars.
- Walk to colored circles.
- Find hidden objects.

---

# Example Scene

The player reaches a broken bridge.

A beaver says:
"I need 7 logs. I already have 4."

Question:
How many more?

Correct answer repairs the bridge.

Player chooses:
- Cross bridge
- Explore cave
- Help beaver gather more supplies

Each choice changes future scenes.
